How it works

Three steps, and everyone's grocery spend lives in one place.

1

Create your household

Open Settings → Create household, give it a name like "The Gurney Family," and you're the owner.

2

Share your invite code

SlipSide generates a unique code. Send it to whoever you shop with — a partner, roommate, or parent.

3

They join, spend flows in

Anyone who enters the code sees the same shared budget. Their scans and orders count toward it automatically.

The details

Who can see what?

Every household member sees the shared budget and combined category spend. Individual till slips are tied to whoever scanned them.

How many people can join?

Add as many people as your household actually has — partners, flatmates, family. No extra cost per person.

Can I leave a household?

Yes, from Settings at any time. Your own spend history stays with you.

Does this need the Pro plan?

Yes, household budgeting is a Pro feature. Every new user gets a free 1-month Pro trial, so you can try it out before deciding.
